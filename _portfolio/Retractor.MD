# Purpose

To accurately reverse engineer the mechanical design of a Weitlaner Retractor and develop a functional prototype that demonstrates the instrument’s key mechanical features and operation.

---

# Features

* Recreated the gear feature found on the original retractor as part of the reverse-engineered design
* Modeled two opposing edges designed to replicate the retractor’s tissue-engaging geometry.
* Reproduced the overall opening and closing configuration of the original instrument.
* Developed detailed SolidWorks models, assembly, and engineering drawings based on caliper measurements.
* Created a physical prototype to assess dimensional accuracy, fit, and overall mechanical performance.
